# PLACE

> PLACE, Inc. is a real estate technology and business-services company founded in 2020 by Ben Kinney and Chris Suarez, headquartered in Bellingham, Washington. It sells an end-to-end operating platform to real estate teams, agents and brokerages — CRM, lead-generation websites, transaction management, automated marketing, mobile apps and AI tooling — bundled with shared back-office services (accounting, hiring, training, finance, advertising) and adjacent mortgage, title and escrow, insurance, property management and home-services offerings.

## Status of the API surface

PLACE publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no SDK, no webhooks and no machine-readable specification**. The platform is delivered only to contracted teams behind single sign-on. As of 2026-08-05 the only machine-readable documents PLACE serves publicly are OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect discovery metadata from its sign-on hosts.
